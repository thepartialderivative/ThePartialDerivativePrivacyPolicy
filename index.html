At a glance. MathPhysicsShopManager is a private, local tool operated by The Partial Derivative for the owner's authorized business accounts. We do not sell account data, collect social media passwords, or offer public account registration.

01 About the application

The Partial Derivative operates MathPhysicsShopManager to manage its artwork and business accounts. Its intended social media use is to schedule and publish artwork chosen by the owner, using only accounts the owner explicitly authorizes. This policy covers the application and this privacy page.

Current functionality: Pinterest supports OAuth authorization and local credential storage. Pinterest account and board retrieval, Pin publishing, and automatic scheduling are not yet enabled. Instagram supports an authorized account check and an individually approved test post. Any future Pinterest scheduling will require the owner to choose each Pin for publication.

The application runs on the owner's Windows computer. It is not a service for connecting other people's accounts and is not directed to children.

02 Information accessed

  • Authorization information: app credentials, OAuth access tokens, a Pinterest refresh token when returned, granted permissions, and token expiry information. Authorization codes are used to complete the connection and are not saved as account records.
  • Pinterest permissions: the app requests user_accounts:read for account identification, boards:read for selecting a destination board, and pins:write for publishing owner-selected artwork. These are requested capabilities; the current version does not retrieve or store Pinterest profiles, boards, or Pins.
  • Instagram information: account ID, username, account type, permission status, and, after an approved test post, the media ID, permalink, media type, and publishing status. Profile checks are saved locally.
  • Owner-provided content: artwork, captions, product descriptions, local drafts, and publishing records. These are business materials supplied or prepared by the owner, rather than content collected from Pinterest.
  • Other shop connections: the same local application can access the owner's authorized Etsy and Printify shop, product, listing, and limited order summaries. Those summaries omit customer names, email addresses, shipping addresses, and messages.
  • Privacy correspondence: if you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include.

The social integrations do not request direct messages, contact lists, followers, or unrelated account activity. Sign-in and consent take place with the social platform; the application does not ask for or store social media passwords.

03 How information is used

Information is used to connect and verify the owner's accounts, prepare artwork, perform explicitly authorized operations, check their results, and resolve connection or publishing problems. Privacy correspondence is used to respond to the request.

Approved publishing sends the selected artwork, caption, and required account information to the relevant platform. Instagram may fetch an approved image from a public image URL supplied by the owner. Published content is then subject to that platform's visibility settings and privacy practices.

We do not sell or rent account information, use it for targeted advertising, or share Pinterest API information with third parties. The application does not use platform data to train AI models. It does not collect Pinterest data for cross-platform profiling or unrelated analytics. Pinterest and Instagram process information under their own privacy policies.

04 OAuth tokens & security

App secrets and OAuth tokens are stored in a local credential vault encrypted with Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI), tied to the owner's Windows account. The app has no plaintext credential fallback. Tokens are used to authenticate directly with the relevant platform and are not included in this website or its public repository.

The local app accepts connections only from the same computer. It uses HTTPS for its local browser interface and platform requests. Temporary session and OAuth cookies support local sign-in security. API access logging is disabled to avoid recording authorization callback codes.

Local artwork, drafts, and the shop database are not encrypted by the application. Their protection also depends on Windows account access, device security, and any backups maintained by the owner. No storage or transmission method provides an absolute security guarantee.

05 Data retention

  • Credentials and tokens remain in the encrypted vault until replaced or removed through the local disconnect command. Token expiry does not automatically erase the stored record.
  • Pinterest API content is not currently retrieved or retained. When account or board reads are implemented, information will be requested as needed rather than stored, in accordance with Pinterest's developer guidelines.
  • Instagram profile checks, local artwork, drafts, and publishing records have no automatic deletion schedule in the current version. They remain until the owner removes them from local storage.
  • Etsy and Printify snapshots expire after one hour and are purged on application startup, dashboard access, or a successful sync. If the app is stopped, an expired snapshot can remain on disk until the app runs again.
  • Privacy correspondence is kept as needed to respond to and document a request, and to meet any applicable legal obligations.

06 Disconnecting & deleting data

The owner can use the app's forget pinterest or forget instagram command to remove that platform's locally saved app credentials and OAuth tokens. This stops further authenticated use from that local connection.

Local disconnection does not revoke access at the platform. To revoke authorization, remove the application in the platform's connected-app or permissions settings. Revoking access there also does not automatically erase local files.

Disconnecting does not automatically delete saved Instagram profile checks, artwork, drafts, publishing records, or owner-maintained backups. For deletion of these records, or to request access or correction of information we hold about you, email thepartialderivative@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request — MathPhysicsShopManager.” We will verify the request as needed and handle applicable requests without undue delay and within legally required time limits. Please do not send passwords or access tokens.

Deleting local records does not remove already published posts. Manage or delete those through Pinterest or Instagram. The platforms' own retention policies apply to information they hold.

07 This privacy website

This is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, forms, or cookies to this page. Visiting it does not connect a social media account or provide access to the private application.

GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes as described in its GitHub Pages documentation and Privacy Statement. Emailing us is handled through your email provider and our Gmail inbox.
